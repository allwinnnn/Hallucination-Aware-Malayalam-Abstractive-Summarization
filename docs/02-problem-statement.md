# Problem Statement

The increasing volume of electronic text creates a need for automatic summarization systems that can reduce lengthy documents into concise representations.

Malayalam presents additional challenges for abstractive summarization because of its morphologically rich linguistic structure.

Although multilingual pretrained language models have expanded the capabilities of NLP systems for Indian languages, Malayalam-to-Malayalam abstractive summarization remains comparatively underdeveloped.

The study therefore investigates the ability of multilingual models to generate Malayalam summaries and examines the effect of zero-shot prompting, few-shot prompting, and supervised fine-tuning.
