# Zero-Shot Results

The zero-shot configuration evaluates the ability of pretrained multilingual models to perform Malayalam summarization without supervised task-specific adaptation.

The zero-shot results show low lexical overlap across the evaluated models.

For ROUGE-1, mBART obtained the highest score among the zero-shot configurations with a score of 1.43%.

For BERTScore, Sarvam obtained the highest zero-shot score of 81.61%.

The results indicate that pretrained multilingual models can produce semantically related outputs, but direct zero-shot inference provides limited lexical matching with the reference Malayalam summaries.
