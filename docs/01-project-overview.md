# Project Overview

Malayalam-to-Malayalam abstractive summarization remains a challenging task due to the linguistic characteristics of Malayalam and the requirement to generate concise summaries while preserving the information present in the source.

This study presents a comparative framework for evaluating multilingual language models for Malayalam abstractive summarization.

The framework investigates three inference and training configurations:

- Zero-shot
- Few-shot
- Fine-tuned

Four multilingual models are analyzed:

- mBART
- mT5
- BLOOMZ
- Sarvam

The study evaluates both summary quality and factual consistency.
